They come from more than one visit. The map marks the places that can be located from the films; it is not a record of one continuous drive.

The films

Place by place

The coast and the mountain

Halkidiki

Gerakini is named as his favorite beach in Greece, and the place where he grew up. The summer camp film is set in Agios Nikolaos, Halkidiki, where he spent childhood summers. The Mount Itamos hike is listed in the order of that film’s chapters.

1Gerakini

Halkidiki, Greece

The film Swimming in Gerakini, Greece | Crystal Clear Water & Marine Life is described as his favorite beach in all of Greece and the place where he grew up, with a chapter titled “Life under the sea.”

His favorite beach in Greece, and the place where he grew up. The film looks at clear Aegean water, schools of fish, rocky outcrops, and soft sand.

2Summer camp at Agios Nikolaos

Halkidiki, Greece

The film ΧΑΝΘ Κατασκήνωση Αγίου Νικολάου | My Summer Camp Tribute in Halkidiki is described as a tribute to the place where he spent childhood summers, with beaches, paths, and memories, at Agios Nikolaos, Halkidiki.

A tribute to the ΧΑΝΘ camp where he spent childhood summers — beaches, paths, and friendships. The pin marks the village named in the film; the exact camp grounds are not given.

3Mount Itamos

Sithonia, Halkidiki

The film Mount Itamos, Greece | Hiking to an Ancient Tree describes a hike on Mount Itamos (Dragoudeli) in Sithonia, Halkidiki, to the ancient Itamos tree. The short A Greek Mountain Overlooking the Aegean shows a view from Mount Itamos toward the Aegean Sea.

The mountain of the hike, with forest paths and views toward the Aegean.

4Profítis Ilias Church

Mount Itamos (Dragoudeli), Halkidiki

The Mount Itamos film has a chapter titled Profítis Ilias Church. The short Mount Dragoudeli, Greece | Profítis Ilias Church describes a stop at Profítis Ilias Church on Mount Dragoudeli in Halkidiki.

The first stop on the Mount Itamos hike. A short was also filmed at the church on Mount Dragoudeli, which the long film treats as the same mountain.

–Pyrofylakio / Fire Tower

Mount Itamos, Halkidiki

The Mount Itamos film has a chapter titled Pyrofylakio / Fire Tower, and its description includes the fire tower on the route.

The second chapter of the Mount Itamos hike. A precise point for the tower is not given, so it is not marked separately; the map pin for the mountain is nearby.

–The ancient Itamos tree

Mount Itamos, Halkidiki

The Mount Itamos film has a chapter titled Itamos Tree. The short The Ancient Itamos Tree | Halkidiki, Greece describes a closer look at the ancient Itamos tree from the Mount Itamos hike.

The hike’s destination, also seen in a Short. The exact tree is not separately mapped; the mountain pin is nearby.
