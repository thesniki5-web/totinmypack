Hi, I’m Teddy. Totin My Pack is where I share the places, roads, and moments I want to remember—and hope you’ll enjoy discovering too.

My travels take me through Greece and across the United States, from Texas back roads and Route 66 to scenic parks, historic sites, small towns, and quiet spots in nature. I make short films about the places I visit, along with Shorts that capture memorable views, wildlife encounters, and roadside surprises.

Here on the website, you can browse films by destination, follow trips, and find restaurants and hotels connected to the journeys. Whether you’re planning a visit, remembering a place you’ve been, or simply in the mood for a little armchair travel, I’m glad you stopped by.

Pack along and explore.

— Teddy
