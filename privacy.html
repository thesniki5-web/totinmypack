Privacy

This site uses GoatCounter to count visits. It does not have an email signup or a contact form.

GoatCounter is a privacy-friendly analytics service. Its own documentation says it does not track personal data: it does not store cookies, localStorage, or other data in your browser; it does not store IP addresses or a tracker ID; and it does not share information with third parties. It is not used here for advertising. You can read GoatCounter’s privacy documentation.

Visit counts for this site are public and can be read without signing in.
