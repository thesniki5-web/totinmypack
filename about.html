About

Travel with Totin My Pack through Greece and across the United States. We share short travel films, scenic drives, state parks, historic places, and personal memories from our trips.

The channel began on September 24, 2022. The films are short — most run a few minutes — and follow scenic drives, state parks, historic places, and personal memories from the road.

Places in the films include Halkidiki and a 2008 trip to Skopelos, Meteora, and Mount Olympus; Texas parks, churches, and river country; Dallas wildlife and weather, including the total solar eclipse of April 8, 2024; Route 66; the Ozarks and the Great Plains; the Southeast and Mid-Atlantic; California’s redwoods and coast; the Southwest; and a 2012 winter film of Berlin.

Watch the films, and subscribe for new ones, on YouTube at @totinmypack.
